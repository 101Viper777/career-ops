# Story Bank — Master STAR+R Stories

This file accumulates your best interview stories over time. Each evaluation (Block F) adds new stories here. Instead of memorizing 100 answers, maintain 5-10 deep stories that you can bend to answer almost any behavioral question.

## How it works

1. Every time `/career-ops oferta` generates Block F (Interview Plan), new STAR+R stories get appended here
2. Before your next interview, review this file — your stories are already organized by theme
3. The "Big Three" questions can be answered with stories from this bank:
  - "Tell me about yourself" → combine 2-3 stories into a narrative
  - "Tell me about your most impactful project" → pick your highest-impact story
  - "Tell me about a conflict you resolved" → find a story with a Reflection

## Stories

### [Product Strategy] Pivoted edFlow to leadership analytics

**Source:** Master CV — edFlow (Mid 2024 to Present)  
**S (Situation):** We started with AI exam creation and marking, but the path to reliable revenue was slower than expected.  
**T (Task):** Decide whether to stay with the original product direction or pivot to a faster value proposition.  
**A (Action):** I led the decision process with my co-founder, reviewed customer signals, prioritised a leadership-facing analytics experience, and redirected delivery to the new scope.  
**R (Result):** We moved from a slower adoption path to a more commercially practical direction focused on leadership data use-cases.  
**Reflection:** Good product work is not only building features. It is making hard calls early when market feedback does not support the original plan.  
**Best for questions about:** product judgement, ambiguity, startup pressure, strategic decision-making

### [Leadership] Managed and mentored a 4-intern team

**Source:** Master CV — edFlow (Mid 2024 to Present)  
**S (Situation):** We had limited startup resources and needed to deliver across product and engineering with interns at different skill levels.  
**T (Task):** Build a team rhythm that kept delivery moving while lifting intern capability.  
**A (Action):** I set clear priorities, broke work into practical deliverables, taught implementation patterns on the go, and reviewed outputs regularly to keep quality high.  
**R (Result):** The team shipped core components while developing confidence and better technical independence over time.  
**Reflection:** Leadership is consistency and clarity. People grow faster when expectations and feedback loops are explicit.  
**Best for questions about:** people leadership, mentoring, delegation, team coordination

### [Technical Delivery] Built an AI-enabled education stack end-to-end

**Source:** Master CV — edFlow (Mid 2024 to Present)  
**S (Situation):** We needed a production-capable stack that could support rapid feature changes in a small team.  
**T (Task):** Deliver reliable end-to-end implementation across frontend, backend, and cloud operations.  
**A (Action):** I implemented and coordinated a Next.js frontend, Firebase backend and hosting, Google Cloud services, serverless functions, Pub/Sub workflows, and CI/CD practices.  
**R (Result):** We maintained a working platform that supported experimentation, iteration, and product changes without a large engineering team.  
**Reflection:** End-to-end ownership matters in lean teams. A simple, maintainable architecture often wins over over-engineering.  
**Best for questions about:** system design, delivery under constraints, cloud implementation, DevOps mindset

### [Stakeholder Communication] Translated AI for non-technical audiences at UTS

**Source:** Master CV — UTS AI Ambassador (May 2024 to Present)  
**S (Situation):** Many students and visitors had a narrow view of AI as only chatbot tools.  
**T (Task):** Explain AI in a way that was engaging, practical, and easy to understand.  
**A (Action):** I ran interactive sessions and examples covering search ranking, fraud detection, and real-world AI systems, adjusting explanations based on audience background.  
**R (Result):** Sessions were more interactive, and attendees left with a clearer understanding of practical AI applications.  
**Reflection:** Communication quality is a force multiplier. Technical depth only creates value if people can understand and act on it.  
**Best for questions about:** communication, customer education, developer advocacy, cross-functional collaboration

### [Client Problem Solving] Diagnosed business ops bottlenecks and proposed automation

**Source:** Master CV — Independent AI and Automation work  
**S (Situation):** Business owners had growth targets but recurring process issues were slowing execution.  
**T (Task):** Identify high-friction operational points and design practical automation options.  
**A (Action):** I mapped current workflows, highlighted priority bottlenecks, and proposed actionable automation steps (including enriched outreach and personalised email flows).  
**R (Result):** Recommendations created a clearer operations roadmap and were later adopted to improve execution quality.  
**Reflection:** Great technical solutions start with business diagnosis. The strongest outcomes come from solving the right problem first.  
**Best for questions about:** client engagement, consulting mindset, automation design, business impact

### [Project Leadership] Led a 7-person capstone team for an AI tutor

**Source:** Master CV — Capstone Project (Mar 2024)  
**S (Situation):** The team needed to deliver an AI tutor for Stage 6 Maths with multiple integrated components.  
**T (Task):** Coordinate team delivery while maintaining technical cohesion across features.  
**A (Action):** I led planning and execution, aligned team members to clear modules, and integrated key agents for tutoring, progress tracking, data collection, and question generation.  
**R (Result):** Delivered a functional AI tutoring solution covering the intended learning workflow.  
**Reflection:** Clear ownership boundaries and integration checkpoints are critical when multiple contributors are building connected AI features.  
**Best for questions about:** teamwork, project delivery, ownership, collaboration under deadlines
