# Mode: contacto - LinkedIn Power Move

1. **Identify targets** via WebSearch:
   - hiring manager for the team
   - assigned recruiter
   - 2-3 peers in similar roles

2. **Select a primary target**: the person who would benefit most from hiring the candidate.

3. **Generate message** using a 3-sentence structure:
   - **Sentence 1 (hook):** one specific point about company context or current AI challenge
   - **Sentence 2 (proof):** strongest quantified candidate achievement relevant to this role
   - **Sentence 3 (proposal):** low-pressure ask for a short chat

4. **Versions**:
   - EN (default)
   - ES (only when relevant for Spanish-language companies)

5. **Alternative targets** with short reasoning for each backup option.

**Message rules:**
- maximum 300 characters (LinkedIn connection request limit)
- no corporate-speak
- avoid generic filler lines
- include one reason that invites a real response
- never share phone number
